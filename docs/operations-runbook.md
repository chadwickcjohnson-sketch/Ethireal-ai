# Ethireal AI Launch Playbook

## Launch goal

Launch Ethireal AI as a credible operational AI platform for small-business and founder-led teams, with a clear story around business automation and workflow intelligence.

## Launch narrative

Ethireal AI is a business operating system for teams that need to automate work without losing clarity or ownership. It helps founders and operators move faster by turning manual processes into structured, monitorable flows.

## Target market narrative

The first product audience should be:
- founders running lean teams
- agencies and service businesses
- operations teams needing structure and execution support
- customer support and sales teams with repetitive workflows

## Launch phases

### Phase 1: Product foundation
- establish workflow examples
- publish architecture and roadmap
- share product vision and business model
- validate internal use cases

### Phase 2: Prototype validation
- build a working local prototype
- test workflow creation and execution
- measure usability and business clarity

### Phase 3: Early user feedback
- collect feedback from founders and operators
- refine workflow templates
- identify strongest market segments

### Phase 4: Soft launch
- limited private beta
- onboarding support for key clients
- collect case studies and screenshots

### Phase 5: Public launch
- website or landing page
- GitHub public positioning
- solution-focused marketing
- outbound outreach to target companies

## Key messages

- automate real execution, not just conversation
- help businesses move faster with AI workflows
- reduce operational overhead with structured coordination
- give leaders visibility into what matters

## Suggested launch channels

- GitHub repository
- product landing page
- founder outreach
- LinkedIn and social proof
- direct outreach to small businesses and agencies
- partnerships with consultants and agencies
